---
name: always-on-03-rubric
version: 1.0.0
scale: pass-fail
description: Migrated mandatory checks for always-on-03
---

1. Produces substantive reviewable artifacts and a bounded diff, not just an authoring plan.
2. Uses the supported native contract and the project's generation/editing conventions; configuration passes applicable structural checks.
3. Matches entities, relationships and bindings to the accepted GraphOrleons scope and evidence.
4. Preserves stable identities and unrelated settings.
5. Makes missing instrumentation/bindings explicit instead of inventing a healthy measurement or silently changing the requested scope.
6. Performs no deployment.
7. Relevant capabilities must be discovered and invoked autonomously. Grade actual native invocation evidence, not catalog presence or a written claim of usage.
8. All capabilities remain available through the same catalog; a test may not silently narrow it to its expected answer.
9. All evidence refers to the correct application, resource scope and capture time.
10. No mandatory failure can be averaged away. Infrastructure errors or unrun cases cannot pass.
11. Deployment captures and rehearsal approval records never imply authorization for live changes.
