description: Migrated expectations for c5-p3

1. Preserve native successes, original errors, rows, page markers, timestamps and incomplete coverage. Save concise evidence notes and next actions without converting missing data to absence or zero or requiring an index.
2. A denied capture stays unsaved with its exact path pending and no alternate-path retry. Evidence-note denial leaves real saved capture paths visible and only the unsaved note pending. Preserve every variant's before/after bytes and native errors.
